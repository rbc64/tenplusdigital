# Drop new thumbnails here

1. Click **Add file → Upload files** (top right of this folder).
2. Drag in your new thumbnails (PNG or JPG, up to 100 at a time).
3. Click **Commit changes**.

About a minute later they appear at the top of https://tenplusdigital.com/portfolio/,
converted to fast-loading WebP and filed by date. The originals are removed from
this folder automatically once they're processed.

**Dates:** keep naming files the way you already do, e.g. `WIFE_-A-Nov-06.png`.
The year is the year you upload it. For older work, add the year to the name:
`WIFE_-A-Nov-06-2025.png`.
